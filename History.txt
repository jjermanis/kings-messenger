Version 01.07	29 minutes dev	0 minutes CPU	3 minutes test		3 hours 18

Version 01.09	45 minutes dev	2 minutes CPU	3 minutes test		4 hours 50

One of the things needed to make a "real" version of the game is to have a way
to great real level. With ChatGPT, we decided to create a separate app to design
levels. We worked together on decisions. Then... I could not get him to generate
code. It would repeatedly state what it would do, but wouldn't. Apparently, the
AI technical term for this is "Laziness". I'd estimate it was 4 chats in about
20 minutes to get a design planned. The next 6 chats were no new info, just a 
summary of what it would do, but did not. It went to the limit of tokens - I had
to wait to finish.
After that, I went through thre stages that worked fairly well. There was a defect:
the terrain would not show the map in the UI. ChatGPT was able to resolve that.
This is a nice start. You can see the terrain and edit. You can see what's there.
There are several TODOs: scrolling, placing enemies, saving. But this is a nice
substantial start.
